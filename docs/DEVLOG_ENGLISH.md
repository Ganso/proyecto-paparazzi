# PhotoHacks now speaks English

Hi everyone,

PhotoHacks is now available in English. Version 0.6.0 translates the whole game: every menu, every Academy lesson, all thirty arcade levels, the report on every photo, the on-screen help and even the changelog of every past version.

{IMG:idioma}

### Pick your language

The first time you launch the game, it asks which language you want. After that you can switch any time in Options, and the game reloads in the other language right away. If you already had PhotoHacks installed, it will ask you once too.

### The same tone, in another language

From day one, the idea behind PhotoHacks has been to teach photography the way Spanish YouTuber Jaime Altozano teaches music: with examples you can see and touch, with a bit of humor, and without cutting corners on the real thing. I've tried hard to keep that tone in English. The Academy still tells you that a photo is a bucket of light, and the tutor still has a couple of jokes up its sleeve.

{IMG:academia}

The translation goes beyond the text. Key names, controller buttons and touch gestures, cameras, lenses and focus modes all speak your language, and so do the people you're looking for: Your next assignment might be a stocky adult in a black shirt and a red beret, with a navy shoulder bag.

{IMG:encargo}

### What is PhotoHacks?

For those of you arriving now: PhotoHacks is a game for learning photography by taking photos. You get an assignment, you look for the person among the crowd in the park and you take their picture. You choose the framing, the focus and the exposure, and the game hands you back the developed photo with a report that explains what went well and what didn't.

{IMG:resultado}

- **Four cameras**, each with its own viewfinder: a compact, a rangefinder, an SLR and a waist-level TLR.
- **The Academy**: ten lessons with theory, a demo, a practice and an exam with a report from the tutor.
- **The arcade**: thirty levels, from your first point-and-shoot assignment to backlit portraits, panning and night shots.
- **Sandbox** and **a big park** you can stroll through freely, camera in hand.

### Also new in 0.6.0

- The Options menu is reorganized in pairs: language and theme, gear and exposure steps, look and rumble, album and badges.
- A few small fixes in the photo report and the on-screen help.

### Free, offline and open

PhotoHacks follows the same philosophy as everything I make at GeeseBumps: It's free, with no ads, no microtransactions and no accounts, and it works completely offline. You can download it for Windows, Linux and macOS, play it right here in your browser, or try the experimental Android version. The code is open source on GitHub.

If you find a sentence that sounds off in English, please tell me in the comments: I'd love to fix it.
