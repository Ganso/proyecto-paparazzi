## Level 1 · First assignment

### arcade_nivel_1_titulo · title
First assignment

### arcade_nivel_1_texto · briefing
Find the person and take their photo. The compact does everything.

## Level 2 · Up close

### arcade_nivel_2_titulo · title
Up close

### arcade_nivel_2_texto · briefing
They must fill at least 60 % of the photo's height: Use the zoom.

## Level 3 · All alone

### arcade_nivel_3_titulo · title
All alone

### arcade_nivel_3_texto · briefing
Nobody else must show up in the frame.

## Level 4 · Two's company

### arcade_nivel_4_titulo · title
Two's company

### arcade_nivel_4_texto · briefing
Exactly one more person in the frame, within 90 seconds: Wait for them to cross paths.

## Level 5 · Golden ratio

### arcade_nivel_5_titulo · title
Golden ratio

### arcade_nivel_5_texto · briefing
Put their face on a golden line, not in the center.

## Level 6 · Telephoto

### arcade_nivel_6_titulo · title
Telephoto

### arcade_nivel_6_texto · briefing
Shoot at 135 mm or more. With that much zoom they won't fit whole: Frame the face.

## Level 7 · Room ahead

### arcade_nivel_7_titulo · title
Room ahead

### arcade_nivel_7_texto · briefing
Wide angle, at 35 mm or less, and leave room on the side they're walking toward: A photo breathes where its subject is heading.

## Level 8 · Blurred background

### arcade_nivel_8_titulo · title
Blurred background

### arcade_nivel_8_texto · briefing
Aperture priority: You pick the aperture ({diafragma}) and the camera does the rest. Open it up to blur the background.

## Level 9 · Sharp face

### arcade_nivel_9_titulo · title
Sharp face

### arcade_nivel_9_texto · briefing
They say you should focus on the eyes. Our mannequins don't have any, so we'll settle for the face: Put the single-point AF right on it.

## Level 10 · Freeze the runner

### arcade_nivel_10_titulo · title
Freeze the runner

### arcade_nivel_10_texto · briefing
Shutter priority: You pick the time ({velocidad}) and the camera does the rest. A runner with a telephoto needs 1/2000 s or faster; the more zoom, the faster.

## Level 11 · Manual focus

### arcade_nivel_11_titulo · title
Manual focus

### arcade_nivel_11_texto · briefing
The rangefinder has no autofocus: Overlap the double image (wheel or R/T). Exposure is still automatic and people walk slower.

## Level 12 · Big park

### arcade_nivel_12_titulo · title
Big park

### arcade_nivel_12_texto · briefing
Find your subject while you stroll, with nobody else in the photo.

## Level 13 · What they do

### arcade_nivel_13_titulo · title
What they do

### arcade_nivel_13_texto · briefing
Your subject sits on a bench to read, check their phone, drink coffee or feed the birds. Photograph them while they do it, with a sharp face (still no eyes).

## Level 14 · Everything sharp

### arcade_nivel_14_titulo · title
Everything sharp

### arcade_nivel_14_texto · briefing
Your subject and the bandstand in one photo, both sharp: Stop down ({diafragma}) and wait for them to walk past it.

## Level 15 · All manual

### arcade_nivel_15_titulo · title
All manual

### arcade_nivel_15_texto · briefing
All manual for the first time: The exposure starts out well metered. Adjust it with the light meter. And get close: The subject must fill at least half the photo.

## Level 16 · Blue hour

### arcade_nivel_16_titulo · title
Blue hour

### arcade_nivel_16_texto · briefing
Low light: Aperture priority. Wide-open aperture and manual focus on the face, which is where the eyes would be.

## Level 17 · Spot-on exposure

### arcade_nivel_17_titulo · title
Spot-on exposure

### arcade_nivel_17_texto · briefing
All manual, with clouds: Leave the light meter at zero, off by a quarter stop at most. Here the dials move in thirds: With full stops it would be down to luck.

## Level 18 · Backlit

### arcade_nivel_18_titulo · title
Backlit

### arcade_nivel_18_texto · briefing
The sun behind your subject: The camera meters that sun and leaves the face dark. Face the sun, spot-meter on your subject and compensate toward +2 ({compensacion}).

## Level 19 · The silhouette

### arcade_nivel_19_titulo · title
The silhouette

### arcade_nivel_19_texto · briefing
Same sun, opposite intent: Your subject in black against the sky. Facing the sun, expose one to two stops below what the light meter asks for.

## Level 20 · Starry night

### arcade_nivel_20_titulo · title
Starry night

### arcade_nivel_20_texto · briefing
Stop down to f/11 or more and the lights become stars. Get 1/30 s or faster: You'll have to play with the setting you can spare. That's slower than a 50 mm can hold: Hold your breath and find someone still.

## Level 21 · Waist level

### arcade_nivel_21_titulo · title
Waist level

### arcade_nivel_21_texto · briefing
You look down into the TLR and the viewfinder swaps left and right. Everything is manual, no rush.

## Level 22 · Mirror

### arcade_nivel_22_titulo · title
Mirror

### arcade_nivel_22_texto · briefing
Golden ratio in the square, with the image flipped.

## Level 23 · 6×6 portrait

### arcade_nivel_23_titulo · title
6×6 portrait

### arcade_nivel_23_texto · briefing
Portrait at sunset: Sharp face (the loupe will show you there are no eyes), blurred background and a big subject. Open the aperture and compensate with the shutter speed.

## Level 24 · Runner in the mirror

### arcade_nivel_24_titulo · title
Runner in the mirror

### arcade_nivel_24_texto · briefing
Freeze a runner with the TLR: You need 1/1000 s, its fastest speed.

## Level 25 · Last light

### arcade_nivel_25_titulo · title
Last light

### arcade_nivel_25_texto · briefing
A single shot, 60 seconds, a sharp face and nobody else.

## Level 26 · The pan

### arcade_nivel_26_titulo · title
The pan

### arcade_nivel_26_texto · briefing
The pan: Set 1/30 s, hold {girar} toward where they run and wait. The camera matches their pace (“Tracking subject”): Shoot without letting go. Runner sharp, background streaked. With mouse or pad, it's all your hand.

## Level 27 · The light trail

### arcade_nivel_27_titulo · title
The light trail

### arcade_nivel_27_texto · briefing
The opposite of panning: Camera still and a slow speed, from 1/30 s to 1/8 s (at this hour they won't burn the photo). Let go of the turn keys: The park comes out sharp and the runner, a trail.

## Level 28 · Signature portrait

### arcade_nivel_28_titulo · title
Signature portrait

### arcade_nivel_28_texto · briefing
With the 105 mm: Blurred background and the person on the golden ratio.

## Level 29 · Walking the dog

### arcade_nivel_29_titulo · title
Walking the dog

### arcade_nivel_29_texto · briefing
Find whoever's walking the dog: Both in the frame and both sharp, all manual. The owner must fill at least 40 % of the height.

## Level 30 · Nocturne

### arcade_nivel_30_titulo · title
Nocturne

### arcade_nivel_30_texto · briefing
Two shots, at night and all manual: Put the focus point on the face. Make it big, with nobody else.
