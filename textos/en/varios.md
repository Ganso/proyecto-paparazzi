### mirar_y_encontrar_arrastra_horizontalmente_o_usa_a_d_y_las_flech
LOOK AND FIND
Drag sideways or use A/D and the arrows. W/S or wheel: Zoom.
Click on the scene: Picks the nearest AF point and focuses.
F: Focus again · 1–9: AF point · G: Thirds grid.

ADJUST AND SHOOT
Q/E: Aperture · Z/X: Shutter speed · C/V: ISO · R/T: Focus distance.
You can also click or drag the values on the top bar. Right-click: Decrease.
Space: Shoot · Enter: Continue · Esc: Close help · ?: Open help.

ON A TOUCH SCREEN
One finger: Pan with inertia · Pinch: Zoom · Two fingers up and down: Manual focus.
A tap: AF point and focus · Drag a setting to change it.

READ THE LIGHT
Needle at 0: Correct exposure. Positive ΔEV: Dark photo. Negative: Too bright.
At long focal lengths, use shorter times. For more sharpness, focus and stop down.
The camera stays at 1.60 m: At 24 mm the adults on the 4 m lane fit.
