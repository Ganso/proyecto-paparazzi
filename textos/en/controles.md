### ayuda_texto_teclado
Look: Drag with the mouse (the view goes where you move it) or ⟦←⟧⟦→⟧⟦↑⟧⟦↓⟧ · ⟦A⟧⟦D⟧: Continuous turn.
Zoom: Wheel or ⟦W⟧⟦S⟧, zoom lenses only.
AF: Click, ⟦F⟧ or FOCUS. Area AF picks the nearest surface among nine points. ⟦1⟧–⟦9⟧ picks the point.
MF: ⟦Shift⟧ + wheel, ⟦R⟧⟦T⟧ or the slider. With a prime lens the wheel alone works too.
SLR: Line up the two halves of the circle. Rangefinder: Overlap the double image.
Manual exposure: ⟦Q⟧⟦E⟧ aperture, ⟦Z⟧⟦X⟧ shutter speed, ⟦C⟧⟦V⟧ ISO. Mode A: You pick the aperture. Mode S, the time.
Automatic with compensation: ⟦[⟧⟦]⟧ or the wheel over the AUTO button.
⟦Space⟧ shoot · ⟦G⟧ thirds · ⟦Tab⟧ controls over the viewfinder · ⟦I⟧ on-screen help.
⟦Y⟧ lowers the camera to search and brings it back to your eye (classic park).
TLR: You look down into it and the viewfinder swaps left and right. ⟦L⟧ 3× loupe · ⟦K⟧ crank (sandbox, 12 shots).
Arcade: Each level sets the camera, shots, time, minimum score and conditions.
With a controller plugged in, this help shows the controller and its buttons.

### ayuda_texto_tactil
Look: Drag a finger across the picture.
Zoom: Pinch with two fingers, or pick Zoom on the strip below and use − and +.
Focus: A tap on the person, or the “AF” button. A long tap focuses and locks focus and exposure.
Shoot: The ● button. Pressing it focuses and releasing it shoots. If you slide your finger off, it doesn't shoot.
Camera settings: Tap one on the strip below and change it with − and +, or slide your finger over it.
Search with your own eyes: “Camera” lowers it and raises it again.
On the left: Thirds, focus and exposure lock, and metering mode.
Big park: The stick walks and dragging your finger looks around. “Run” and “Crouch” stay on.
