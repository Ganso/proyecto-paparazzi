### tutorial_bienvenida
Welcome to PhotoHacks. You're in the middle of a park with a camera: Your job is to find the person in each assignment and take a good photo of them. Let's go step by step. Press {aceptar} or “Start”.

### tutorial_mirar
Look around with {mirar}: To the sides, and up and down too.

### tutorial_zoom
Zoom in ({zoom}) past 70 mm, then zoom back out below 40 mm.

### tutorial_bajar
With a long lens it's hard to find anyone. Lower the camera to search with your own eyes ({bajar}) and raise it again. A tip: Behind the fence there are only extras. Whoever you're after will always be closer.

### tutorial_af
Focus on a person: Aim one of the boxes at them and use autofocus ({af}). You'll hear a beep when it locks.

### tutorial_ayuda
The panel on the left is the on-screen help: Your controls, their value and whether you're in charge (MAN) or the camera is (AUTO). Hide it and show it again with {ayuda_pantalla}.

### tutorial_disparar
Take a photo of anyone you like: {disparar}. Then you'll see it developed, with its score.

### tutorial_seguir · Tracking someone with the keys (keyboard only)
Follow someone who's walking: Hold {girar} toward where they're going. The camera waits for them to reach the focus point and matches their pace: You'll see “Tracking subject”. That's how pans are done.

### tutorial_encargo
A real assignment: The description is at the top. Lower the camera ({bajar}) to find that person and raise it for the photo. If there's too much on screen, press {ayuda_pantalla} to clear it.

### tutorial_controles
The manual controls work three ways. Pick one with {elegir_tab} and change it with {cambiar_pag}. Click it and use the mouse wheel. Or use its direct shortcut. You'll find the shortcuts with {ayuda}.

### tutorial_diafragma
Aperture priority (A): You're in charge of the aperture. Change it twice: Pick it on the strip below and turn the wheel, or use its shortcut, {diafragma_y}.

### tutorial_tercios · Tip after changing the aperture; works for keyboard, controller and fingers
A tip: In Options you can switch “Exposure steps” to thirds for finer control. The same controls then move a third of a stop; with {paso_entero}, a full stop.

### tutorial_abruma
Just a reminder: If you see too much information, you can toggle the help with {ayuda_pantalla}. The strip below stays: Click whatever you want to change, then the wheel. It only shows what you can change at that moment.

### tutorial_mf
Manual focus: Pick Focus on the strip and turn the wheel, or use its shortcut ({enfoque_mf_zoom}), until the two halves of the center circle line up on someone.

### tutorial_controles_mando
The manual controls are on the strip below. Pick one with {elegir_control} and change it with {cambiar_control}. Zoom and manual focus are also on the right stick. You can check every button with {ayuda}.

### tutorial_diafragma_mando
Aperture priority (A): You're in charge of the aperture. Change it twice: Pick it on the strip below with {elegir_control} and change it with {cambiar_control}.

### tutorial_abruma_mando
Just a reminder: If you see too much information, you can toggle the on-screen help with {ayuda_pantalla}. The strip below stays, and it only shows the controls you can change at that moment.

### tutorial_mf_mando
Manual focus: Turn the ring with {enfoque_mf_zoom} until the two halves of the center circle line up on someone.

### tutorial_paseo
This is the big park, and here you walk. With the camera lowered, walk with {andar} and look with {mirar_paseo}. Walk a few meters.

### tutorial_sacar
To take photos you have to raise the camera: {sacar}. You can't walk with it at your eye: You walk and search with the camera lowered, and raise it only to shoot. Raise it.

### tutorial_foto_paseo
Take a photo of anyone you like ({disparar}), then lower the camera ({sacar}) so you can keep walking.

### tutorial_resultado_paseo
Done. Now lower the camera to keep walking.

### tutorial_mf_tactil

### tutorial_paseo_tactil

### tutorial_sacar_tactil
