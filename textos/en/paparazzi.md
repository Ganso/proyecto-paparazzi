## Tarjetas de novedad

### pz_nov_bienvenida_titulo
Paparazzi

### pz_nov_bienvenida_texto
You'll be asked for photos of people in the park: Find them, frame them the way they say and shoot. The camera focuses and exposes by itself.

### pz_nov_entero_titulo
Full shot

### pz_nov_entero_texto
From head to toe, nothing cut off. It shows the whole person: What they wear, how they move.

### pz_nov_semaforo_titulo
The corners

### pz_nov_semaforo_texto
When the corners turn green, the photo is good. In amber, they tell you what's missing.

### pz_nov_enganche_titulo
Lock-on

### pz_nov_enganche_texto
Aim at someone and zoom in: The camera locks on and follows them. To let go, look away or zoom all the way out.

### pz_nov_comprobar_titulo
Is it them?

### pz_nov_comprobar_texto
With someone locked on, the card shows in green what matches the person you're after. All green means it's them.

### pz_nov_medio_titulo
Medium shot

### pz_nov_medio_texto
Cut between the chest and the waist. It's the shot for conversations: You see the face and the hands.

### pz_nov_reloj_titulo
The clock

### pz_nov_reloj_texto
Every good photo gives you time. If it hits zero, the run is over. While you read the card, the clock waits.

### pz_nov_congelar_titulo
Joker: Freeze

### pz_nov_congelar_texto
With {pz_congelar} the park stands still for five seconds. Use it when the person is getting away.

### pz_nov_primer_plano_titulo
Close-up

### pz_nov_primer_plano_texto
Head and shoulders. It's the shot for emotion: The face is what matters.

### pz_nov_otro_titulo
Joker: Another request

### pz_nov_otro_texto
With {pz_otro} you swap the request for another. You'll earn more at the milestones.

### pz_nov_hito_titulo
Milestone

### pz_nov_hito_texto
Pass this level and next time you can start from the next one, without repeating the earlier ones.

### pz_nov_cara_titulo
Extreme close-up

### pz_nov_cara_texto
Just the face: It can cut the hair, but never the chin. It needs lots of zoom: Look on the nearby paths.

### pz_nov_fallo_titulo
Missed photos

### pz_nov_fallo_texto
From now on, every photo that doesn't count costs you %d seconds. Check the corners before you shoot.

### pz_nov_sola_titulo
Alone

### pz_nov_sola_texto
Nobody else recognizable in the shot. Wait for others to move away or tighten the frame with the zoom.

### pz_nov_reportaje_titulo
Photo story

### pz_nov_reportaje_texto
Three photos in a row of the same person, from the widest to the tightest.

### pz_nov_grande_titulo
The big park

### pz_nov_grande_texto
Here you walk with {andar}. The zoom wheel brings the camera up and {sacar} puts it away to keep walking.

### pz_nov_zona_titulo
The area

### pz_nov_zona_texto
The card tells you where the person is and how far. If you take too long, the arrow guides you.

### pz_nov_americano_titulo
Cowboy shot

### pz_nov_americano_texto
Cut between the hip and the knee. It comes from westerns: That's how you saw the gun.

### pz_nov_general_titulo
Wide shot

### pz_nov_general_texto
The person small and the place big: It shows where they are.

### pz_nov_corte_limpio_titulo
Clean cut

### pz_nov_corte_limpio_texto
Never cut at a joint (neck, waist, knees, ankles): It looks like an amputation. Cut well and you earn %d s.

### pz_nov_acompanada_titulo
With company

### pz_nov_acompanada_texto
They want them with other people: Exactly as many as the card says.

### pz_nov_frente_titulo
Facing and in profile

### pz_nov_frente_texto
Facing means looking toward you; in profile, side on. Get ahead on the path and wait for them.

### pz_nov_perro_titulo
With their dog

### pz_nov_perro_texto
The dog they're walking has to be in the shot too.

### pz_nov_comprobar_lenta_titulo
Checking takes longer

### pz_nov_comprobar_lenta_texto
Now the card takes a moment to show what matches: Hold the lock-on for a bit.

### pz_nov_lugar_titulo
The place

### pz_nov_lugar_texto
Show where they are: The bandstand or the water has to be in the photo.

### pz_nov_actividad_titulo
Doing something

### pz_nov_actividad_texto
Show them busy: Reading, on their phone, with a coffee or feeding the pigeons.

### pz_nov_corredor_titulo
Runners

### pz_nov_corredor_texto
They cross the frame in a moment: Shoot runners always full length.

### pz_nov_dobles_titulo
Watch for look-alikes

### pz_nov_dobles_texto
Some people are dressed almost the same. Compare every trait before you shoot.

### pz_nov_enganche_lento_titulo
Slow lock-on

### pz_nov_enganche_lento_texto
The camera takes a little longer to lock on: Keep the person in the middle.

### pz_nov_dos_mods_titulo
Two demands

### pz_nov_dos_mods_texto
Some photos ask for two things at once. The corners won't turn green until both are met.

### pz_nov_noche_titulo
At night

### pz_nov_noche_texto
In low light, colors fool you: Look for people under the streetlights.

### pz_nov_sin_comprobar_titulo
By eye

### pz_nov_sin_comprobar_texto
The card no longer tells you if it's them: Trust your eyes.

### pz_nov_encuadras_tu_titulo
You frame it

### pz_nov_encuadras_tu_texto
The lock-on follows the person, but you choose the cut: Tilt up or down to place them.

### pz_nov_infinito_titulo
No end

### pz_nov_infinito_texto
From here on everything mixes and each stretch is a little harder. How far can you go?

### pz_nov_flecha_titulo
The arrow

### pz_nov_flecha_texto
If you take too long, an arrow on the edge shows where to look. A little later, a ring marks the person.

### pz_nov_taparse_titulo
They covered up

### pz_nov_taparse_texto
If you get too close with the camera, people cover their faces. A good street photographer zooms from afar.

### pz_nov_rescate_titulo
The rescue

### pz_nov_rescate_texto
Time's up, but you have a rescue left: %d more seconds, once per run.
