Options menu reorganized, and on a controller the D-pad and left stick go through every option.

A 30-level arcade in six blocks: Backlight, silhouette, light trails, everything sharp, what people do… Your progress is kept.

### novedades_0_4_0_2 · What's new in 0.4.0
New sound across the whole game: Shutters, dials, footsteps, chatter, animals and an ambience for each light of the day.

### novedades_0_4_0_3 · What's new in 0.4.0

### novedades_0_5_0_1 · What's new in 0.5.0
Far more realistic photos: Real panning and light trails, optical blur on every camera, camera shake, noise and grain.

### novedades_0_5_0_2 · What's new in 0.5.0
Hold the turn key toward where someone is going: The camera waits for them, matches their pace and tells you (“Tracking subject”).

### novedades_0_5_0_3 · What's new in 0.5.0

### novedades_0_5_1_1 · What's new in 0.5.1

### novedades_0_5_1_2 · What's new in 0.5.1

### novedades_0_5_2_1 · What's new in 0.5.2

### novedades_0_5_3_1 · What's new in 0.5.3
The light meter no longer gets distracted: Matrix metering follows whoever you frame or focus on, even if the point lands just beside them.

### novedades_0_5_3_2 · What's new in 0.5.3
Arcade reviewed level by level: No assignment asks for what its camera, light or film can't do.

### novedades_0_5_3_3 · What's new in 0.5.3
Panning moves to golden hour, light trails to blue hour and the TLR portrait to sunset: Each photo with its own light.

### novedades_0_5_4_1 · What's new in 0.5.4
Manual focus like a real camera: Whoever walks in front shows up split in the viewfinder, and what you had in focus stays put.

### novedades_0_5_5_1 · What's new in 0.5.5
Night stars: Stop down to f/11 or more and the streetlights and the bandstand bulbs come out as stars in the photo.

### novedades_0_5_5_2 · What's new in 0.5.5
Arcade: Level 20 is now “Starry night”, a handheld night challenge where you choose who to photograph.

### novedades_0_5_5_3 · What's new in 0.5.5
