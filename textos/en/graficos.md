### gfx_desc_Bajo
Same look at the lowest cost: 50 % resolution (FSR 2), no global illumination or ambient occlusion, and little grass.

### gfx_desc_Medio
70 % resolution, global illumination with 3 cascades, ambient occlusion and 2048 shadows. No volumetric fog.

### gfx_desc_Alto
85 % resolution, full global illumination, volumetric fog, physical penumbra and depth of field in the viewfinder.

### gfx_desc_Ultra
Native resolution with 4× MSAA, screen-space reflections and light bounce, full grass and shadows on every streetlight.

### gfx_integrada
Integrated graphics: The profiles turn global illumination off (it gives a washed-out image). In Custom you can force it on.

### gfx_subtitulo_ligero · Graphics screen on phones and browsers (1160 px at 18 px: two lines)
On phones and in the browser the game uses a lighter renderer. The profile changes shadows, smoothing and detail: If it runs slowly, go lower.

### aviso_rendimiento · Notice over the viewfinder when the game runs below 42 FPS (700 px at 16 px: one line)
The game is running slowly on this computer: Try a lower profile in Options → Graphics.
